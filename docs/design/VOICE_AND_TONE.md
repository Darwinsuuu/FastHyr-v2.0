# Voice and tone

We sound like a **good interviewer**: curious, specific, never a gatekeeper. Warm and professional on every surface; more reassuring on candidate screens, more efficient on TA screens.

## Rules
- **Sentence case** everywhere (buttons, headings, tabs). Uppercase only in `label` eyebrows.
- **No emoji, no exclamation marks** in product copy.
- Say **"AI interview"** plainly — don't hide it, don't hype it. Never "our algorithm", "our AI has analysed your profile".
- Name: **FastHyr** (capital F and H, one word). The assistant is **Ava**; the interviewer is **the AI interviewer**.
- Talk to candidates as **"you"**, about **their work**. Always say **what happens next** and who they'll hear from ("You'll hear from them, not from us").
- Give TA teams **evidence, not verdicts**: "No evidence yet of people management", never "weak leader".
- Numbers explain themselves: "91 / 100 · 21 points above your pass mark".
- Errors say what happened and what to do; they keep the user's work: "We couldn't save that answer. It's still here — try again."
- Destructive confirmations say exactly what happens and what doesn't ("Pipelines, candidates and results stay exactly as they are").
- Buttons are verb phrases: "Start my interview", "Advance candidate", "Buy 250 credits". Never "Submit", "OK", "Click here".

## Word list
| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| Submit application | Send application |
| Rejected | Not moving forward |
| Candidate score: 62 (alone) | 62 / 100 · 8 below your pass mark |
| Failed résumé screen | Résumé not a match |
| Job / requisition / opening | Pipeline (TA side) · role (candidate side) |
| Users / seats | Members |
| Tenant / org | Workspace |
| Oops! Something went wrong | We couldn't load this. Try again in a moment. |
| AI decided | FastHyr recommends · Your team decides |

## Candidate-side reassurance lines (reuse)
- "This is a conversation, not a test. Take your time before answering."
- "Ask the interviewer to repeat or rephrase anything."
- "Your name, photo, address, age and graduation year are never used to review your résumé or score your interview. A person on the {Company} team makes every hiring decision."

## Email tone
Short paragraphs, first name greeting, one clear link, sign-off from "The {Company} hiring team". Rejections thank the candidate, avoid clichés ("we had many strong applicants" is fine once, not twice), include the human-review line.
