# Personas

FastHyr serves two sides of the table. **Default to the TA perspective** unless a surface is candidate-facing.

## 1. TA / HR professional — primary user, the customer
- **Who:** Recruiter or TA lead at a company hiring in volume (tech, BPO, fintech, retail). Posts roles on LinkedIn, JobStreet, Indeed, Kalibrr, Glassdoor, Facebook or a careers page.
- **Goals:** cut time-to-screen, assess every applicant the same way, get structured evidence without reading every résumé or doing every first call.
- **Pains today:** hundreds of résumés per role, phone-screen scheduling, inconsistent screening, no time to reply to everyone.
- **In FastHyr:** Recruiter or Admin role. Creates pipelines, shares apply links, reviews "Ready for review" candidates, rejects or advances, edits email templates, watches credits.
- **Surface style:** data-dense, efficient, evergreen-led.

## 2. Workspace admin — often the same person as #1
- **Goals:** control who sees what, manage billing and credits, keep the employer brand consistent.
- **In FastHyr:** Admin role. Company profile, departments, members, billing, credit usage.

## 3. Hiring manager — secondary user
- **Who:** The team lead who owns the open role.
- **Goals:** see a short list with evidence, decide who to meet next, quickly.
- **In FastHyr (MVP):** Hiring manager role — read-only view of shortlisted candidates (Ready for review, Flagged, Advanced) and their assessments in their departments; downloads reports; asks Ava. Tells the recruiter who to advance.

## 4. Candidate — end user, not the customer
- **Who:** Job seeker anywhere in the world, often applying from a phone, sometimes nervous about AI.
- **Goals:** a fair, clear, low-anxiety process; know what happens next; hear back.
- **In FastHyr:** Signs in with Google or Microsoft, applies with a PDF résumé, takes an async video + voice or voice-only AI interview, gets emails at each step. Never sees scores, signals or flags.
- **Surface style:** warm, spacious, persimmon-led, one primary action per screen, reassuring copy.

## 5. Demo visitor
- A TA professional evaluating FastHyr who tries the public demo interview from the marketing site. Nothing they upload is kept.
